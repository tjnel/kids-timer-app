# Kids Timer — Image Generation Prompts

Every asset targets a **transparent PNG** on a **fully transparent background** (no checkerboard, no solid fill).
Style across all assets: **clean vector-style illustration, soft rounded shapes, thick outlines (2–3 px at render size), pastel-friendly palette that works on light mint/cream/white backgrounds**. Think "modern children's book meets mobile app icon." No pixel art — smooth anti-aliased edges.
